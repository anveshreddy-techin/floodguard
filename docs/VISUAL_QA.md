## 1. Visual Review Checklist

### 1. Viewport Utilization (100vh)
- **Status:** ✅ VERIFIED
- **Review:** The entire viewport is utilized without awkward white space or vertical scroll jumping. The central GIS map fills the primary viewport with floating layer pills and coordinated intelligence panels on the right.

### 2. Elimination of Generic "AI Template" Look
- **Status:** ✅ VERIFIED
- **Review:**
  - Removed uniform card grids. Replaced with unified mission-control panels, vector flow overlays, and contextual slide-in drawers.
  - Eliminated loud sci-fi neon colors. Applied emergency-grade semantic colors (Emerald, Amber, Orange, Rose) on deep navy surfaces (`#070d1e`).
  - Removed decorative empty boxes. Every element presents real, verifiable evidence or telemetry.

### 3. Motion & Animation Quality
- **Status:** ✅ VERIFIED
- **Review:**
  - Stream particle vectors flow smoothly ($40\text{ms}$ refresh) along river channels from ridge to valley.
  - High and Extreme risk zones pulse gently without distracting the operator.
  - Village intelligence drawer slides in from the right edge with a clean $300\text{ms}$ easing.
  - No abrupt layout shifts on focus or layer toggle.

### 4. Accessibility & Legibility
- **Status:** ✅ VERIFIED
- **Review:**
  - High contrast text (`#f1f5f9` on `#070d1e`) across all telemetry numbers.
  - Status indicators use both color and explicit textual labels (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`, `ONLINE`, `BLOCKED`).
  - Strict truthfulness labeling: all synthetic and simulation modes are visibly badged.
