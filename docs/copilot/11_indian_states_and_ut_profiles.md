# Disaster & Flood Profiles: 28 Indian States & 8 Union Territories

This document serves as the authoritative geographical and hydrological reference for FloodGuard AI Copilot across all administrative territories of India.

---

## Himalayan & Hilly States (High Flash-Flood & Landslide Risk)

### 1. Uttarakhand (UK)
- **Primary River Basins**: Alaknanda, Bhagirathi, Mandakini, Yamuna, Kali, Ganga, Ramganga.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Uttarkashi, Pithoragarh, Bageshwar, Nainital, Dehradun.
- **Primary Hazards**: Cloudbursts, Flash Floods, Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs), Landslides, River Damming/Debris Flows.
- **Critical Thresholds**: Rainfall > 50 mm/h or > 100 mm/24h on steep slopes (> 25°); Mandakini warning at Rudraprayag (624.0m), danger at 626.0m.
- **Key Historical Events**: 2013 Kedarnath Chorabari lake breach, 2021 Chamoli Rishiganga rock avalanche & flash surge.

### 2. Himachal Pradesh (HP)
- **Primary River Basins**: Beas, Sutlej, Ravi, Chenab, Yamuna.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Kullu, Mandi, Shimla, Kinnaur, Lahaul-Spiti, Kangra, Chamba.
- **Primary Hazards**: Cloudburst flash floods, landslide dam breaches, riverbank erosion, high-altitude snowmelt floods.
- **Critical Thresholds**: Beas at Pandoh Dam warning/danger levels; heavy rainfall accumulation (> 60 mm in 3 hours).
- **Key Historical Events**: July-August 2023 Beas basin catastrophic flooding, 2000 Sutlej valley flash floods.

### 3. Jammu & Kashmir (JK - UT)
- **Primary River Basins**: Jhelum, Chenab, Tawi, Indus, Kishanganga.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Srinagar, Anantnag, Pulwama, Baramulla, Jammu, Rajouri, Poonch.
- **Primary Hazards**: Riverine valley overflow, urban flooding, cloudbursts in tributary nullahs (e.g., Baltal/Amarnath).
- **Critical Thresholds**: Jhelum gauge at Sangam (Alert 18 ft, Warning 21 ft, Danger 23 ft); Ram Munshi Bagh (Warning 18 ft, Danger 21 ft).
- **Key Historical Events**: September 2014 catastrophic Jhelum basin inundation; 2022 Amarnath cloudburst.

### 4. Ladakh (LA - UT)
- **Primary River Basins**: Indus, Shyok, Zanskar, Suru.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Leh, Kargil.
- **Primary Hazards**: Cloudbursts in hyper-arid cold desert valleys, glacial lake outburst floods, debris avalanches.
- **Critical Thresholds**: Short-duration localized rain > 15 mm/h causing rapid mudslides in sediment-rich alluvial fans.
- **Key Historical Events**: August 2010 Leh flash flood / mudflow disaster.

### 5. Sikkim (SK)
- **Primary River Basins**: Teesta, Rangeet.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Mangan (North Sikkim), Gyalshing, Namchi, Gangtok, Pakyong.
- **Primary Hazards**: GLOF, moraine-dammed lake failure, cloudburst-induced debris flows, NH-10 arterial cutoffs.
- **Critical Thresholds**: South Lhonak lake level fluctuations; Teesta gauge at Chungthang and Singtam exceeding danger marks.
- **Key Historical Events**: October 2023 South Lhonak GLOF destroying Chungthang dam; June 2024 North Sikkim landslides.

### 6. Arunachal Pradesh (AR)
- **Primary River Basins**: Siang, Subansiri, Kameng, Lohit, Dibang, Tirap.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Upper Siang, East Siang (Pasighat), West Kameng, Kurung Kumey, Dibang Valley.
- **Primary Hazards**: Flash floods from transboundary Tibetan river surges (Yarlung Tsangpo), mega-landslides, river blockages.
- **Critical Thresholds**: Siang river stage spikes (> 1.5 m/h rate of rise).

## North-Eastern States (Monsoon Floodplains & Tributary Flash Floods)

### 7. Assam (AS)
- **Primary River Basins**: Brahmaputra, Barak, Subansiri, Dhansiri, Kopili, Manas, Jia Bharali.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Cachar, Karimganj, Hailakandi, Dhemaji, Barpeta, Morigaon, Nagaon, Kamrup.
- **Primary Hazards**: Chronic multi-wave riverine inundation, tributary flash floods, embankment breaches, wetland siltation.
- **Critical Thresholds**: Brahmaputra at Guwahati/Dibrugarh crossing Danger Level; Barak at Annapurna Ghat > 19.83m.
- **Key Historical Events**: 2022 Silchar (Cachar) submergence; annual Brahmaputra valley floods affecting Kaziranga.

### 8. Meghalaya (MG)
- **Primary River Basins**: Umngot, Simsang, Myntdu, Umiam, tributaries draining south to Bangladesh plains.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: East Khasi Hills (Sohra/Cherrapunji), South West Khasi Hills, West Garo Hills.
- **Primary Hazards**: Extreme world-record precipitation (> 500 mm in 24h), flash floods in southern valleys, hill collapses.

### 9. Manipur (MN)
- **Primary River Basins**: Imphal, Iril, Thoubal, Barak.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Imphal East, Imphal West, Bishnupur, Thoubal, Tamenglong.
- **Primary Hazards**: Valley basin waterlogging, Loktak lake buffer saturation, hill landslides along NH-37.

### 10. Nagaland (NL)
- **Primary River Basins**: Doyang, Dhansiri, Dikhu, Tizu.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Dimapur, Kohima, Wokha, Mokokchung, Phek.
- **Primary Hazards**: Flash floods in foothill valleys (Dimapur), highway mudslides, slope subsidence.

### 11. Mizoram (MZ)
- **Primary River Basins**: Tlawng, Chhimtuipui, Tuirial.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Aizawl, Lunglei, Champhai, Kolasib.
- **Primary Hazards**: Steep slope debris slides, river choke surges, monsoon flash flows.

### 12. Tripura (TR)
- **Primary River Basins**: Gumti, Howrah, Manu, Khowai, Muhuri.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: South Tripura, Gomati, West Tripura (Agartala), Unakoti.
- **Primary Hazards**: Transboundary river inundation, flash flooding in Gumti basin, urban waterlogging.
- **Key Historical Events**: August 2024 severe statewide flooding and landslides.

## Western Ghats & Coastal States (High Intensity Monsoon & Inundation)

### 13. Kerala (KL)
- **Primary River Basins**: Periyar, Bharathapuzha, Pamba, Chaliyar, Meenachil, Chalakudy, Iruvazhinji/Chaliyar headwaters.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Wayanad, Idukki, Ernakulam, Pathanamthitta, Alappuzha, Thrissur, Kottayam.
- **Primary Hazards**: Catastrophic debris flows, tea-estate slope liquefaction, reservoir emergency spillway discharge, coastal backwater floods.
- **Critical Thresholds**: 24h rainfall > 204 mm (Extremely Heavy); Idukki / Mullaperiyar dam full reservoir levels (FRL).
- **Key Historical Events**: August 2018 statewide disaster; July 2024 Wayanad (Chooralmala & Mundakkai) debris avalanches.

### 14. Maharashtra (MH)
- **Primary River Basins**: Krishna, Godavari, Koyna, Mula-Mutha, Vashishti, Ulhas, Mithi (Mumbai).
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Mumbai Suburbs, Ratnagiri, Raigad, Kolhapur, Sangli, Satara, Pune, Chiplun.
- **Primary Hazards**: Coastal estuarine flash floods (Chiplun/Mahad), urban cloudbursts (Mumbai), Krishna basin inundation.
- **Critical Thresholds**: 200 mm/24h in Western Ghats catchments; Vashishti river tidal synchronization.
- **Key Historical Events**: July 26, 2005 Mumbai cloudburst (944 mm in 24h); July 2021 Chiplun & Taliye disaster.

### 15. Karnataka (KA)
- **Primary River Basins**: Cauvery, Krishna, Tungabhadra, Sharavathi, Netravati, Kali.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru, Shivamogga, Dakshina Kannada, Belagavi, Bagalkote, Bengaluru Urban.
- **Primary Hazards**: Ghat landslides (Kodagu), Krishna basin backwater floods (Belagavi), urban storm drain inundation (Bengaluru).

### 16. Goa (GA)
- **Primary River Basins**: Mandovi (Mahadayi), Zuari, Chapora.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: North Goa (Sattari, Bicholim), South Goa.
- **Primary Hazards**: Estuarine high-tide river backflows, mining basin runoff surges.

### 17. Tamil Nadu (TN)
- **Primary River Basins**: Cauvery, Adyar, Cooum, Vaigai, Tamirabarani, Bhavani.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Chennai, Kanchipuram, Tiruvallur, Cuddalore, Thoothukudi, Tirunelveli, Nilgiris.
- **Primary Hazards**: Northeast Monsoon (Nov-Dec) coastal depressions/cyclones, Nilgiris ghat landslides, urban drainage choke.
- **Key Historical Events**: December 2015 Chennai flood disaster; December 2023 South Tamil Nadu extreme rainfall.

## Indo-Gangetic Plains & Central Basins (Riverine Inundation & Breaches)

### 18. Bihar (BR)
- **Primary River Basins**: Ganga, Kosi ("Sorrow of Bihar"), Gandak, Burhi Gandak, Bagmati, Kamla Balan, Mahananda, Son.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Supaul, Saharsa, Madhepura, Khagaria, Samastipur, Darbhanga, Muzaffarpur, Patna.
- **Primary Hazards**: Cross-border upstream surges from Nepal, sediment-driven channel avulsion (Kosi), embankment breaches.
- **Critical Thresholds**: Kosi barrage discharge > 300,000 cusecs; Ganga at Digha Ghat and Gandhi Ghat crossing Danger Levels.
- **Key Historical Events**: August 2008 Kosi embankment breach at Kusaha (Nepal).

### 19. Uttar Pradesh (UP)
- **Primary River Basins**: Ganga, Yamuna, Ghaghara (Sarayu), Gomti, Ramganga, Rapti, Sharda, Gandak.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Gorakhpur, Bahraich, Lakhimpur Kheri, Ballia, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Siddharthnagar.
- **Primary Hazards**: Prolonged monsoon floodwave transit, Sharda riverbank erosion, Rapti flash surges.

### 20. West Bengal (WB)
- **Primary River Basins**: Bhagirathi-Hooghly, Teesta, Torsa, Damodar, Mayurakshi, Rupnarayan, Subarnarekha.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Jalpaiguri, Alipurduar, Cooch Behar, Malda, Murshidabad, Howrah, Hooghly, South 24 Parganas.
- **Primary Hazards**: North Bengal foothill flash floods (Duars), Damodar Valley dam releases, Sundarbans tidal surges.

### 21. Odisha (OR)
- **Primary River Basins**: Mahanadi, Brahmani, Baitarani, Subarnarekha, Rushikulya, Vansadhara.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Cuttack, Puri, Kendrapara, Jagatsinghpur, Balasore, Bhadrak, Jajpur, Sambalpur.
- **Primary Hazards**: Deltaic flooding, Hirakud dam emergency gate operations, Bay of Bengal cyclone storm surges.
- **Critical Thresholds**: Hirakud reservoir level approaching 630.0 ft; Mahanadi discharge at Mundali weir > 1,000,000 cusecs.

### 22. Andhra Pradesh (AP)
- **Primary River Basins**: Godavari, Krishna, Penna, Nagavali, Vamsadhara.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: East Godavari, West Godavari, Konaseema, Vijayawada (NTR district), Alluri Sitharama Raju.
- **Primary Hazards**: Godavari delta surges (Dowleswaram Barrage), Budameru rivulet flash flood in Vijayawada, cyclones.
- **Key Historical Events**: September 2024 Vijayawada Budameru flash flood inundation.

### 23. Telangana (TS)
- **Primary River Basins**: Godavari, Krishna, Manjeera, Musi, Munneru.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Bhadradri Kothagudem, Mulugu, Khammam, Mahabubabad, Hyderabad.
- **Primary Hazards**: Godavari peak flood at Bhadrachalam (1st Warning 43ft, 2nd Warning 48ft, Danger 53ft), Musi urban floods.

### 24. Gujarat (GJ)
- **Primary River Basins**: Narmada, Tapi, Sabarmati, Mahi, Vishwamitri.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Vadodara, Surat, Bharuch, Junagadh, Jamnagar, Kutch.
- **Primary Hazards**: Sardar Sarovar / Ukai dam releases, Vishwamitri river overflowing into Vadodara city, Arabian Sea cyclones.
- **Key Historical Events**: August 2024 Vadodara urban flood (Vishwamitri overflow).

### 25. Madhya Pradesh (MP)
- **Primary River Basins**: Narmada, Chambal, Betwa, Son, Tapti, Ken.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Hoshangabad (Narmadapuram), Jabalpur, Gwalior, Sheopur, Bhind, Morena.
- **Primary Hazards**: Flash floods from dam spillways (Bargi, Tawa, Bansagar), Chambal ravines overflow.

### 26. Rajasthan (RJ)
- **Primary River Basins**: Chambal, Luni, Banas, Mahi, Ghaggar.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Kota, Jhalawar, Baran, Dholpur, Barmer, Jalore.
- **Primary Hazards**: Sudden high-intensity desert flash floods, Chambal barrage discharge in Hadoti region.

### 27. Punjab (PB)
- **Primary River Basins**: Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Ghaggar.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Ropar, Ferozepur, Patiala, Sangrur, Fazilka.
- **Primary Hazards**: Seasonal torrents (choes), Ghaggar river breach, Bhakra / Pong dam downstream surges.

### 28. Haryana (HR)
- **Primary River Basins**: Yamuna, Ghaggar, Tangri, Markanda.
- **Key Vulnerable Districts**: Ambala, Yamunanagar, Karnal, Panipat, Kurukshetra, Gurugram.
- **Primary Hazards**: Hathnikund Barrage releases into Yamuna, seasonal Shivalik foothill flash floods.

## Union Territories

### 29. Delhi (NCT)
- **Primary River Basin**: Yamuna.
- **Key Vulnerable Areas**: Yamuna floodplain, Kashmere Gate, Civil Lines, Mayur Vihar.
- **Critical Thresholds**: Yamuna Old Railway Bridge (Warning 204.50m, Danger 205.33m).
- **Key Events**: July 2023 record water level (208.66m) surpassing the 1978 peak.

### 30. Chandigarh (CH)
- **Primary Streams**: Sukhna Choe, Patiala Ki Rao.
- **Hazard**: Sukhna lake spillway release, seasonal torrent overflows.

### 31. Puducherry (PY)
- **Hazard**: Sankaraparani and Pennaiyar river floods, coastal cyclone surges.

### 32. Andaman & Nicobar Islands (AN)
- **Hazard**: Tropical depression rainfall, tsunami risk, localized stream flooding.

### 33. Lakshadweep (LD)
- **Hazard**: Coastal storm surges, swell waves, monsoon high-tide inundation.

### 34. Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (DNHDD)
- **Hazard**: Daman Ganga river surge, Madhuban Dam discharge.
