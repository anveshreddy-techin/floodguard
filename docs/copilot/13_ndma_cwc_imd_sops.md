# Official Disaster Protocols: NDMA, IMD, CWC Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

This document contains authoritative administrative SOPs, alert thresholds, and operational command hierarchies governing disaster response across India.

---

## 1. India Meteorological Department (IMD) Classification Standards

### Rainfall Intensity Categories (24-Hour Accumulation):
- **Very Light Rain**: $0.1 - 2.4\text{ mm}$
- **Light Rain**: $2.5 - 15.5\text{ mm}$
- **Moderate Rain**: $15.6 - 64.4\text{ mm}$
- **Heavy Rain**: $64.5 - 115.5\text{ mm}$
- **Very Heavy Rain**: $115.6 - 204.4\text{ mm}$
- **Extremely Heavy Rain**: $\ge 204.5\text{ mm}$
- **Cloudburst Definition**: Precipitation $\ge 100\text{ mm}$ within **1 hour** over a localized geographical area of approximately $20 - 30\text{ km}^2$.

### IMD 4-Stage Color-Coded Warning System:
1. **GREEN (No Warning)**:
   - *Status*: Normal weather conditions; no advisory needed.
   - *Action*: No formal disaster response required.
2. **YELLOW (Watch & Be Updated)**:
   - *Status*: Severely bad weather possible; localized hazards may emerge.
   - *Action*: Keep track of weather bulletins; check emergency equipment.
3. **ORANGE (Alert & Be Prepared)**:
   - *Status*: High probability of severe disruptions, waterlogging, minor landslides, road blockades.
   - *Action*: Mobilize field quick-reaction teams (QRTs); alert vulnerable communities; review shelter availability.
4. **RED (Warning & Take Action)**:
   - *Status*: Extremely dangerous weather; imminent catastrophic flooding, GLOF, or major landslides likely.
   - *Action*: Initiate immediate mandatory evacuation of low-lying floodplains; deploy SDRF/NDRF; close vulnerable roads and bridges.

## 2. Central Water Commission (CWC) River Flood Stages

CWC operates over 1,600 hydrological observation stations across major Indian river basins with clearly defined hierarchical thresholds:

1. **Warning Level (WL)**:
   - The river stage at which floodwater starts spilling over the primary banks into low-lying floodplains.
   - *Protocol*: 24x7 control room activation; patrol embankments; hourly gauge observations.
2. **Danger Level (DL)**:
   - The river stage at which floodwater threatens life, inundates nearby settlements, and tests levee strength.
   - *Protocol*: Sound local sirens; initiate priority evacuation of vulnerable population (infants, elderly, livestock).
3. **Highest Flood Level (HFL)**:
   - The maximum historical water level ever recorded at that specific gauge site.
   - *Protocol*: Extreme emergency; structural emergency measures; aerial reconnaissance; request central military assistance if state resources are overwhelmed.

## 3. National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) Command Hierarchy

### Administrative Framework:
- **National Level**: National Executive Committee (NEC) / NDMA chaired by the Prime Minister; National Crisis Management Committee (NCMC) headed by Cabinet Secretary.
- **State Level**: State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA) chaired by the Chief Minister; State Executive Committee headed by Chief Secretary.
- **District Level (Primary Operational Node)**: District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) headed by the **District Magistrate (DM) / Collector / Deputy Commissioner**.
- **Incident Response System (IRS)**:
  - **Responsible Officer (RO)**: District Magistrate.
  - **Incident Commander (IC)**: Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) or designated senior official at field headquarters.
  - **Operations Section Chief (OSC)**: Oversees search & rescue (NDRF, SDRF, Police, Fire, Home Guards).
  - **Logistics Section Chief (LSC)**: Manages transport, communications, medical supplies, relief camps.
  - **Planning Section Chief (PSC)**: Tracks situational models, GIS flood maps, resource allocation.

## 4. Emergency Escalation & Public Communication Protocol

### Emergency Helpline Numbers:
- **112**: All-India Unified Emergency Response Support System (Police, Fire, Ambulance, Disaster).
- **1070**: State Disaster Management Control Room (SDMA toll-free).
- **1077**: District Disaster Control Room (DEOC toll-free).
- **108**: Emergency Medical Ambulance Service.

### Common Alerting Protocol (CAP - ITU Recommendation X.1303 / standard 1.2):
All public emergency alerts generated by FloodGuard AI for government dissemination conform to CAP 1.2 format:
- `identifier`: Unique message UUID.
- `sender`: Authorized government entity (e.g., `in.gov.ndma.ddma`).
- `sent`: ISO-8601 UTC timestamp.
- `status`: `Actual`, `Exercise`, `System`, `Test`, `Draft`.
- `msgType`: `Alert`, `Update`, `Cancel`, `Ack`, `Error`.
- `scope`: `Public`, `Restricted`, `Private`.
- `info`:
  - `category`: `Geo`, `Met`, `Safety`.
  - `event`: `Flash Flood Warning`.
  - `urgency`: `Immediate`, `Expected`, `Future`, `Past`, `Unknown`.
  - `severity`: `Extreme`, `Severe`, `Moderate`, `Minor`, `Unknown`.
  - `certainty`: `Observed`, `Likely`, `Possible`, `Unlikely`, `Unknown`.
  - `area`: Polygon coordinates of the hazard impact zone.
