# About FloodGuard AI (SIH26192)

## Overview & Mission
FloodGuard AI is a multi-source, hyper-local disaster intelligence and decision-support platform designed specifically for the unique topographical challenges of Indian hilly and mountainous terrain (e.g., Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, and the North-Eastern States).

## Primary System Purpose
Flash floods in steep terrain often occur with lead times of less than 30 to 60 minutes following intense localized cloudbursts, sudden glacial lake outbursts (GLOFs), or colluvial debris blockages. FloodGuard AI continuously integrates multi-source hydro-meteorological data, IoT edge sensors, terrain DEM parameters, and catchment saturation indices to provide early situational awareness.

## Operating Modes
1. **DEMO / SIMULATION MODE**:
   - Uses verified historical hindcasts, deterministic hydrological generators, and synthetic telemetry.
   - All outputs are clearly stamped with `[DEMO]` badges.
   - Public alert dispatching is strictly blocked.
2. **REAL_PILOT MODE**:
   - Direct integration with verified regional AWS stations, CWC gauges, and IoT LoRaWAN mesh networks.
   - Requires institutional authorizations and configured credentials.

## What FloodGuard AI Does NOT Do
- It does **not** issue unreviewed unilateral disaster declarations to the public without human authorization.
- It does **not** fabricate sensor data or pretend offline providers are operational.
- It does **not** replace standard government directives from NDMA, SDMA, or the District Emergency Operation Center (DEOC).
