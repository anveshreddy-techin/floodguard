# Verified Historical Disaster Events (Hindcast Benchmark Archive)

## 1. 2013 Kedarnath / Uttarakhand Multi-Day Disaster
- **Trigger**: Multi-day intense monsoonal rainfall coinciding with Chorabari glacial lake breach.
- **Physical Dynamics**: Extreme river surge in Mandakini and Alaknanda valleys with unprecedented sediment load.
- **Lessons**: Need for antecedent slope saturation modeling and upstream glacial lake screening.

## 2. 2021 Chamoli Rock & Ice Avalanche (Rishiganga)
- **Trigger**: Massive rock and hanging glacier detachment from Ronti peak; non-rainfall onset.
- **Physical Dynamics**: Sudden hyper-concentrated debris flow destroying Tapovan-Vishnugad hydro infrastructure.
- **Lessons**: Early warning requires seismic/acoustic vibration detection and thermal anomaly monitoring.

## 3. 2024 Wayanad Landslide & Flash Surge (Kerala)
- **Trigger**: Prolonged extreme localized rainfall (>300mm in 24h) on steep Western Ghats slopes.
- **Physical Dynamics**: Multiple simultaneous debris flows coalescing in Chooralmala-Meppadi channels.
