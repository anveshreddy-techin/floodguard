# Glossary of Technical & Disaster Management Terms

- **Antecedent Precipitation Index (API)**: A weighted index representing moisture stored in the soil from prior rain events.
- **CAP (Common Alerting Protocol)**: An XML/JSON digital format standard for exchanging public emergency alerts across media.
- **Critical Success Index (CSI)**: Hydrometeorological verification metric: $CSI = \frac{TP}{TP + FP + FN}$.
- **DEM (Digital Elevation Model)**: Topographical raster grid capturing altitude, slope, and catchment drainage paths.
- **GLOF**: Glacial Lake Outburst Flood caused by dam breach or moraine failure.
- **HFL (Highest Flood Level)**: Peak historical water level recorded at a gauging station by CWC.
- **PR-AUC**: Precision-Recall Area Under Curve; primary metric for evaluating imbalanced disaster datasets.
- **PSI (Population Stability Index)**: Statistical metric measuring telemetry distribution shift over time.
- **TWI (Topographic Wetness Index)**: Quantifies topographic control on hydrological processes ($TWI = \ln(a / \tan\beta)$).
