# Citizen Safety Protocols: What to Do During Flash Floods

## 1. Before Flooding (Preparation & Alerts)
- Know your local terrain: identify whether your residence lies in a riverbed concavity, colluvial hollow, or low-lying alluvial terrace.
- Locate nearest designated community shelters (schools, panchayat bhawans, elevated community halls).
- Keep an emergency grab-bag: water purification tablets, dry rations, flashlight, emergency whistle, and identification papers.

## 2. During Sudden Onset Flooding / Cloudburst
- **Move to High Ground**: Do not wait for instructions if you observe rapidly rising turbid water or hear loud roaring upstream (debris flow indicator).
- **Turn Off Utilities**: Disconnect electrical mains and LPG gas valves if safe to do so.
- **Never Walk or Drive in Flood Water**: Just 15 cm of rapid water can knock a person down; 30 cm can float passenger vehicles.
- **Avoid Culverts and Bridges**: Mountain bridges can suffer structural scour or collapse from colluvial battering.
